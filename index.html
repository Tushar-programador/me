The Origin Story

I'm Tushar Kalra, a Software Engineer based in Gurugram with a relentless drive for building backend systems that are reliable, automated, and built to scale.

My journey has been about turning slow, manual business processes into fast, automated systems — invoice extraction with AWS Textract, background job queues with BullMQ and Redis, and REST APIs that connect hardware like barcode scanners directly to production databases.
